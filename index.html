About Marolls Rice Processing Ltd

Processing Quality.
Creating Value.
Feeding the Future.

Marolls Rice Processing Ltd is a Nigerian rice-processing company focused on transforming agricultural produce into quality rice products for consumers and businesses. With its corporate presence in Lekki Phase I, Lagos, and its rice-processing operation in Idjerhe Kingdom, Delta State, Marolls combines commercial reach with a production base positioned within Nigeria's agricultural landscape.

Founded under the leadership of Mr. Tony Maguaje, Marolls was established around a clear opportunity: to participate meaningfully in Nigeria's growing agricultural and food-processing sector by developing a rice business built around processing, quality, distribution and strong market relationships.

Our Philosophy

From paddy to packaged rice, every stage matters.

At Marolls, we believe that the journey from paddy to packaged rice is about much more than simply processing a commodity. It is about creating a reliable food product, developing agricultural value, connecting producers with markets and building a brand that consumers and commercial customers can trust.

From Agricultural Produce to Quality Rice

Transforming agricultural produce into market-ready value.

Rice occupies an important place in Nigeria's food system. It is consumed by households across the country and is an important input for restaurants, hotels, caterers, retailers, distributors and institutions.

Marolls Rice Processing Ltd operates within this ecosystem with the objective of bringing greater value to locally processed rice.

Our approach is centred on the complete processing journey — from the handling of paddy rice through cleaning, processing, sorting, grading and preparation for the market.

Our Processing Base in Delta State

Built around a real processing operation.

Our processing operation is located in Idjerhe Kingdom, Delta State, at Km 2, Urhomehen–Umubumu–Obanaghoro–Emioboma Road.

The Delta State location forms an important part of Marolls' operational identity, placing the company's processing activities within a region with agricultural potential and access to Nigeria's wider food and commercial markets.

From this base, Marolls is building a business capable of participating in the agricultural value chain from raw agricultural produce through processing and ultimately to the consumer.

The Marolls Rice Brand

Premium rice, professionally presented.

Our flagship consumer identity is Marolls Rice, presented as a premium parboiled rice brand.

The brand has been developed to communicate quality, professionalism and confidence. We understand that consumers do not simply buy rice because it is a staple. They also consider cleanliness, appearance, consistency, packaging, availability and the overall trust they place in a brand.

This is why Marolls places emphasis on creating a professional product experience — from processing and sorting through packaging, distribution and customer relationships.

Our ambition is for Marolls Rice to become a familiar and trusted name in Nigerian homes and businesses.

Building the Agricultural Value Chain

Creating value beyond the bag.

One of our broader objectives is to participate in the development of Nigeria's agricultural value chain.

Agriculture creates opportunities at many levels — farming, aggregation, transportation, processing, packaging, distribution, retail and export. Processing companies such as Marolls sit at an important point in this chain because processing can transform agricultural commodities into products with greater commercial value.

Our long-term vision is therefore not limited to selling bags of rice. We want to contribute to an ecosystem in which agricultural production can connect efficiently with modern processing and reliable markets.

Quality & Technology

Modern processing supported by specialised equipment.

Modern food processing increasingly depends on technology and appropriate equipment. Marolls' historical procurement records show investment in specialised rice-processing machinery and equipment from international suppliers, including cleaning, grading, sorting and milling-related machinery, as well as boiler equipment associated with rice-mill operations.

This investment reflects the importance we place on building processing capabilities that can support consistent commercial production.

Technology, however, is only one part of quality. People, processes, sourcing, quality control, packaging, storage and distribution all matter. Our objective is to bring these elements together into one dependable operating system.

Our Vision

A respected Nigerian rice-processing and food brand.

Our vision is to build a respected Nigerian rice-processing and food brand recognised for quality, consistency, professionalism and dependable service.

We want Marolls to grow alongside Nigeria's agricultural economy — creating commercial opportunities, strengthening market connections and developing a brand capable of reaching customers across Nigeria and, ultimately, international markets.
